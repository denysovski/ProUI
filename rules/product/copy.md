# Copy and UX writing (`CP`)

Generated copy is as easy to spot as generated layout. These rules extend the word rules in `SL-37` to
`SL-42` and `SL-71`.

## Voice

- **CP-01 Write like the business talks.** Use the client's own words from their brief, their menus, their
  emails. A bakery does not "empower"; it bakes.
- **CP-02 Specific beats clever.** Name the thing, the number, the place and the time: "Fresh bread from 7:00,
  Vinohradská 12" beats "Crafted with passion".
- **CP-03 No buzzwords.** Avoid "seamless", "unlock", "elevate", "empower", "revolutionise", "next-level",
  "cutting-edge", "game-changing", "supercharge", "effortless", "delve", "leverage", "robust", "in today's
  fast-paced world".
- **CP-04 No slogan rhythm.** Avoid three-word fragments in a row, rhetorical questions as headings, and
  "not X but Y" contrasts (`SL-38`, `SL-71`).

## Clarity

- **CP-05 Short sentences, plain words.** Most sentences under about 20 words; one idea per sentence.
- **CP-06 Front-load.** The important word comes first in headings, buttons and list items, so scanning works.
- **CP-07 Buttons say what happens.** Verb plus object: "Book a table", "Download the menu", "Save changes".
  The button keeps the same words as the heading or link that led to it (`SL-41`).
- **CP-08 Consistent terms.** One name per thing across the whole site ("basket" or "cart", never both).
- **CP-09 Sentence case.** Headings, buttons and labels in sentence case unless the brand insists otherwise.

## UI text

- **CP-10 Error messages: what, why, how to fix.** "This card has expired. Use another card or update the expiry
  date." Never blame the user, never "Invalid input", never only a code.
- **CP-11 Empty states invite.** Say what will be here and how to start (`ST-01`).
- **CP-12 Confirmations name the result.** "Order 4821 is placed. We will email you when it ships." (`ST-10`).
- **CP-13 Permission and data requests explain why.** Say what you need and what the user gets for it, at the
  moment you ask (`PL-04`).
- **CP-14 Honest claims only.** No invented numbers, customers, awards or reviews. Placeholders are marked as
  placeholders for the client to replace (`SL-42` to `SL-44`).
