# Landing page

> The section order a landing page follows, the visitor's question behind each section, and how the call to action repeats. Use it for the home page of a service, SaaS or product site, and for campaign pages.

[Blueprints](README.md) · [Sections](sections.md) · [Page Structure Rulebook](../rules/structure/README.md)

## The order

The order follows the visitor's decision: promise, proof, detail, objections, action. When a section is
present it keeps its place, because each one depends on the one before it. Nine or ten sections is the
upper limit; fewer, well filled, beat more, half filled.

| Section | The visitor asks | It contains | Needed for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header | "Where am I, where can I go?" | logo, four to six links, one primary button (`NV-02`, `NV-05`) | all |
| **Hero** | "What is this, is it for me?" | headline with the outcome, one sentence of how, the primary action, a secondary action, the real product or a real photo | all |
| Proof strip | "Is this legit?" | client logos with permission, a rating with its source, a usage number | when real proof exists |
| Problem | "Do they understand me?" | the situation the visitor is in, in their words | pain-driven offers |
| Product or services | "What does it do, what do I get?" | the offer shown, not listed: screenshots, demo, photos, the services with what each includes | all |
| How it works | "Is it hard to start?" | the steps from first contact to result, named, not numbered (`SL-17`) | services, onboarding-heavy products |
| Detailed features or benefits | "Does it do the thing I need?" | two to four deeper blocks, each with a visual | SaaS, products |
| Deep proof | "Has it worked for someone like me?" | named testimonials, case studies, results with numbers (`SL-43`) | all, when real |
| **Pricing** | "What does it cost?" | packages or tiers, or a "from" price and what changes it ([pricing.md](pricing.md)) | services and products with a price |
| FAQ | "But what about...?" | the five to eight real objections: price, time, contract, support, refund | most |
| **Final CTA** | "I'm ready, what now?" | one sentence, the primary action, what happens after clicking | all |
| **Footer** | "Who are they, how do I reach them?" | contact, links, legal ([footer.md](footer.md)) | all |

## Variations by kind of site

| Site | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Local service (plumber, clinic, salon) | Phone and address in the hero or header; area served; opening hours; reviews from the map listing with a link; booking or call as the primary action |
| Agency or studio | Work comes right after the hero, before services; services short; contact form or "book a call" |
| SaaS | Product screenshot or demo in the hero; proof strip; features with visuals; integrations; pricing preview linking to the pricing page; security note for business buyers |
| Shop landing | Collection or bestseller grid near the top; shipping and returns promise; reviews per product |
| Portfolio | No marketing sections; work first, about and contact after |
| Restaurant | Photos, hours, address and reserve button in the first screen; menu highlights; location |
| Company / B2B | Routes per audience after the hero (customers, partners, careers); industries; references; locations |

## The call to action rhythm

- One primary action for the whole page, worded as the result ("Book a consultation", "Start free trial"),
  not "Submit" or "Learn more" (`CP`).
- It appears in the header, in the hero, after the product or services section, after pricing and in the
  final CTA. Visitors decide at different points; a page with one button at the top loses the ones who
  decide after the proof.
- Secondary actions are quieter (text link or outline button): "See pricing", "View work", "Talk to us".
- On mobile, a sticky bar with the primary action appears once the hero button has scrolled away (`PE-03`).

## What comes after the landing page

The landing page sends people to the pages that close the decision. Each of them repeats the primary action.

| From the landing page | Goes to | That page has |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Services or features section | **Service / feature pages** | full detail, price or "from", one testimonial, CTA |
| Pricing section | **Pricing page** | tiers, comparison table, billing toggle, FAQ, contact for custom needs |
| Proof | **Case studies / work** | one page per project with result |
| "About", footer | **About** | people, story, facts |
| Primary action | **Contact, booking or sign-up** | the shortest form possible, then a thank-you page with what happens next (`PE-07`) |
| FAQ, footer | **Help, FAQ, docs** | answers, then contact |

## Common mistakes

- Features before the visitor knows what the product is.
- Pricing hidden behind "Contact us" for a simple, small-business offer.
- A testimonial wall of unnamed quotes instead of three to five named, specific ones.
- Sections filled with placeholders because "landing pages have them": leave them out.
- A different effect in every section: one signature moment per page (`DC-10`, `EF-02`).
