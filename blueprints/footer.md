# Footers

> Three footer sizes and when each fits. Visitors scroll to the footer on purpose to find contact details, company information and links they missed, so it is navigation, not decoration.

[Blueprints](README.md) · [Navigation Rulebook](../rules/navigation/README.md) (`NV-07`)

## Pick the size from the amount of information

| Footer | When | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slim** | one-page sites, portfolios, apps | name or logo, contact email, two or three legal links, copyright line, social links |
| **Standard** | small business, service, small shop (up to about 15 pages) | brand column with a sentence and contact; two or three link columns; legal row |
| **Fat** | companies, SaaS, shops and anything with many pages or audiences | everything below, grouped into four to six columns |

## Fat footer anatomy

```text
| Brand: logo, one sentence      | Products   | Solutions  | Resources | Company   | Contact          |
| Newsletter (what you get,      | link       | link       | Docs      | About     | phone, email     |
| how often), social links       | link       | link       | Blog      | Careers   | address, hours   |
|                                | link       | link       | Help      | Press     | "Contact us" ->  |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Language / region switch | Payment and trust badges (shops) | Certifications (B2B)                     |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| © 2026 Legal name · Reg. no. · VAT   Privacy · Terms · Cookies · Cookie settings · Accessibility    |
```

- **Columns with headings** that match the header navigation's names (`NV-13`), four to seven links each.
  Show first- and second-level pages only; the full list goes into a sitemap page linked from the footer.
- **Contact** in its own column or the brand column: real phone, email, address, hours.
- **Legal row** last: legal name, registration and VAT numbers where required, privacy, terms, cookie settings,
  accessibility statement, "Report security issues" (`PE-08`, `PE-09`, `PE-15`, `PE-16`, `PE-24`).
- **Status and trust** for products: Status, Security or Trust Center, subprocessors (`PG-23`). The full
  checklist by site type, with the groups seen on Cloudflare, Rox, Heron and xAI, is in
  [legal-and-trust.md](legal-and-trust.md#footer-checklist-from-the-studied-sites).
- **Newsletter** only with a promise: what it contains and how often.
- **Trust elements** that are real: payment methods, certifications, awards.
- **Mobile:** columns become collapsible groups or two columns; contact and legal stay open; tap targets 44 px.

## Avoid

- A footer that repeats the header and nothing else.
- Dozens of unsorted links, keyword lists for SEO, tag clouds.
- Tiny low-contrast text: footer text meets the same contrast as body text (`CL-09`).
- Animated or effect-heavy footers; at most a large brand wordmark in the Modern level.
- "Back to top" as the only content.
