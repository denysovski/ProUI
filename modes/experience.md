# Experience

The work itself leads from the first screen: a portfolio, a campaign, an art piece, a launch. The visitor came
to look and explore.

## May

- use WebGL, scroll-driven sequences, custom cursors and large type, when they show the work
- have one rehearsed focal sequence instead of many small effects
- break the grid deliberately (`LY-10`)

## Must still

- load fast and stay smooth: heavy visuals render only while visible, cap pixel ratio, static fallback
  (`PF-21`, `PF-03`)
- keep content visible without JavaScript and in screenshots (`SL-72`)
- work with reduced motion and keyboard (`AX-12`, `AX-09`)
- let people reach contact or the next project without solving a puzzle

## Rules that weigh most

`SL`, `MO`, `PF-21`, `AX-12`, `GR`, `TY`, `CL`.
