# Persuade

The design is part of what the visitor judges. The style may lead the page, but the offer and the action must
be clear within a few seconds.

## The first screen must

- say what this is and for whom, in a specific headline (`DC-09`, `CP-02`)
- show proof only this business has: the product, the place, the work, a real number (`DC-06`, `SL-45`)
- put the real action in its working form where the category expects it: a booking form, add to cart, a
  search, opening hours and a phone number, not only a "Learn more" link (`PE-02`, `CP-07`)

## The style may

- own the whole surface: its colour as the ground, its type for the headlines (`CL-01` Committed or Drenched)
- shape the composition, as long as navigation still reads as links, text as text and the action as clickable
- carry one authored motion moment (`MO`), never a fade on every block (`SL-35`)

## Watch for

- the template skeleton in disguise: split hero with copy left and image right, then a row of three cards, then
  logos, then pricing (`SL-09`, `LY-12`)
- a style reduced to an accent colour on a neutral template
- a painted "poster" with no working parts (no readable nav, no clear action)

## Rules that weigh most

`SL`, `DC`, `TY`, `CL`, `GR`, `LY`, `CP`, `PE-02`, `PE-03`, `SEO`, `PF-03`.
