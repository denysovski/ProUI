# Style keywords

Named styles I can use in a prompt to say exactly what a site should look like. Each one is a ready set of
palette, type, layout, imagery and motion, built from my Pinterest board (`PINS.md`) and my own projects.

## How to use

- Name one **base style**: `Build a site for a ceramics studio in EDITORIAL CREAM.`
- Add **modifiers** if needed: `BLACK COAL + MEGA TYPE + DITHER GRAIN, maximal.`
- Keywords are case-insensitive; the short id (`black-coal`) works too.
- XSlop still applies. Each style lists the soft XSlop rules it switches on deliberately; everything else stays.
- If a style conflicts with the project's own brand, the brand wins and the style fills the gaps.

## Modifiers

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `MAXIMAL` | more layers, bigger type, denser grids, collage and texture allowed, more motion moments |
| `MINIMAL` | fewer elements, more white space, one image per screen, motion reduced to fades |
| `DARK` / `LIGHT` | forces the base style into a dark or warm-light palette |
| `WARM` / `COLD` | shifts neutrals to cream and stone, or to blue-grey and steel |
| `STILL` / `CALM` / `KINETIC` | motion level: none, slow and subtle, or scroll-driven and expressive |
| `MONO` | removes the accent colour completely |
| `ACCENT <colour>` | replaces the default accent, for example `ACCENT cobalt` |

## BLACK COAL

Keyword: `BLACK COAL` or `black-coal`

Near-black monochrome with one hot accent. Streetwear, studios, nightlife, tech brands with attitude.

- **Palette:** background #0B0B0C, surface #151517, line #26262A, text #EDEDED, muted #8C8C90, one accent (default #FF3B1F)
- **Type:** Heavy condensed or wide grotesk, uppercase display (Clash Display, Cabinet Grotesk); neutral text face (General Sans, Switzer); mono only for codes and prices
- **Layout and imagery:** Full-bleed black and white photography, product grids on black, hairline dividers, tight small labels in the corners
- **Motion:** Slow reveals, hard cuts between sections, hover that inverts black and white
- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-26 (caps labels) allowed
- **Avoid:** glows, purple, gradients, soft rounded cards

## RED SIGNAL

Keyword: `RED SIGNAL` or `red-signal`

BLACK COAL with red as a second voice: black, white and one saturated red used in blocks, not just details.

## EDITORIAL CREAM

Keyword: `EDITORIAL CREAM` or `editorial-cream`

## GALLERY CLASSIC

## POSTER COLLAGE

Keyword: `POSTER COLLAGE` or `poster-collage`

Maximalist print energy: collage, torn paper, stickers, halftone, overlapping type. Events, music, fashion drops, youth brands.

- **Palette:** paper white #F5F3EE, black, plus one or two loud accents (red #E3170A, pink #FF4FA3 or acid #C8FF2E)
- **Type:** Mixed display faces on purpose (grotesk plus a blackletter or stencil), giant numbers, rotated labels
- **Layout and imagery:** Layered collage, cut-out photos, stickers, halftone textures, rule lines, dense poster grids
- **Motion:** Stop-motion style steps, sticker pop-ins, marquee strips
- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-08 (grain/halftone), SL-14 (stickers/pills), SL-26 allowed
- **Avoid:** clean SaaS components, glassmorphism

## SWISS MINIMAL

Keyword: `SWISS MINIMAL` or `swiss-minimal`

Strict grid, lots of white, small precise type. Studios, portfolios, architecture offices, consultancies.

- **Palette:** white #FAFAF8, ink #111111, grey #9A9A9A, one accent used once (signal orange #FF5A1F or blue #2B4BFF)
- **Type:** One grotesk family only (Switzer, General Sans), sizes from a strict scale, left aligned
- **Layout and imagery:** 12-column grid, small text blocks, images aligned to the grid, generous empty fields
- **Motion:** Almost none: fades and underline animations
- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-17 (numbering) allowed when it orders real content
- **Avoid:** decoration of any kind, shadows, gradients
- **Example pins:** [1](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157835225657/) · [2](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157834109158/) · [3](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157833921283/) · [4](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157833921277/) · [5](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157832487251/) · [6](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157832487239/) · [7](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157830869170/) · [8](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157830869167/)

## MEGA TYPE

Keyword: `MEGA TYPE` or `mega-type`

The headline is the image: one or two words so big they run off the edges. Can be combined with any palette.

- **Palette:** follows the base style; usually black or white with one accent
- **Type:** Very large display (12 to 25 vw), tight tracking, cropped at the viewport edges, often uppercase
- **Layout and imagery:** Word as the hero, a single image overlapping the letters, small text tucked into corners
- **Motion:** Letters that slide or scale on scroll, masked reveals
- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-24 (tight tracking) allowed on display sizes only
- **Avoid:** more than one mega headline per screen
- **Example pins:** [1](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157835225661/) · [2](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157834990234/) · [3](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157834990229/) · [4](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157833963461/) · [5](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157833910534/) · [6](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157833910450/) · [7](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157833910417/) · [8](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157832938340/) · [9](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157831026003/) · [10](https://cz.pinterest.com/pin/661255157830935503/)

## LIGHT BEAM

Keyword: `LIGHT BEAM` or `light-beam`

Dark tech and SaaS where the product sits in a beam or glow of light in the brand colour.

- **Palette:** black #07080A, surface #111318, line #1E222A, text #E8EAED, light source in brand colour (orange #FF6A00 or blue #2F6BFF)

- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-02 allowed for the one light source

## VOID PARTICLES

Keyword: `VOID PARTICLES` or `void-particles`

Particle and point-cloud typography on black: generative, techy, music or AI brands.

- **Palette:** black #000000, one neon colour for particles (acid #C8FF2E or teal #2EF2C8), white text

- **Motion:** Particles that drift and react to the cursor, with a static fallback
- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-34 (cursor reaction) allowed for the hero only
- **Avoid:** more than one particle system per page

## CHROME OBJECT

Keyword: `CHROME OBJECT` or `chrome-object`

One glossy 3D centrepiece: chrome, glass or iridescent material on a dark or neutral stage.

- **Palette:** dark stage #0C0C0E or neutral #E9E9EA, object colours come from reflections; one accent for UI

- **Avoid:** multiple 3D objects, objects as wallpaper

## DITHER GRAIN

Keyword: `DITHER GRAIN` or `dither-grain`

Pixel dither, halftone, noise and ASCII textures on images and type. Digital-craft, music, culture, developer brands.

- **Palette:** black #0A0A0A and white, or paper #EDEBE6 and ink; optional single accent
- **Type:** Grotesk or mono display; pixel or bitmap details
- **Layout and imagery:** Dithered photos and shapes, scanline or grain overlays, bitmap icons
- **Motion:** Dither that resolves into the full image on hover or scroll
- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-08 (dithering) and SL-33 (ASCII) allowed
- **Avoid:** using dither on every image

## PRODUCT CLEAN

Light, precise product and app UI: dashboards, fintech, booking, mobile apps.

- **Palette:** white #FFFFFF, surface #F5F6F8, line #E4E6EA, ink #0F1115, one accent (blue #2B5BFF or orange #FF5A1F)

## PLAYFUL POP

Keyword: `PLAYFUL POP` or `playful-pop`

Bright, illustrated and friendly. Kids, food, pets, community products.

- **Palette:** two or three saturated colours (for example #FF6B35, #2EC4B6, #FFD23F) on off-white #FFF9F0
- **Type:** Rounded or soft display (Chillax), friendly text face
- **Layout and imagery:** Illustrations, stickers, rounded shapes, playful photo crops
- **Motion:** Bouncy only on celebratory moments (MO-13 still applies to everyday UI)
- **XSlop rules it allows:** SL-14 (pills) allowed
- **Avoid:** using it for premium or serious brands

## Combinations that work

- `BLACK COAL + MEGA TYPE` — streetwear drop, studio landing page.
- `RED SIGNAL + POSTER COLLAGE, MAXIMAL` — event, festival, music release.
- `EDITORIAL CREAM + GALLERY CLASSIC` — museum, gallery, cultural institution.
- `SWISS MINIMAL + MEGA TYPE` — architecture office or design studio portfolio.
- `LIGHT BEAM + CHROME OBJECT` — developer tool or AI product launch.
- `VOID PARTICLES + DITHER GRAIN, DARK` — music, generative art, creative tech.
- `NOIR PORTRAIT + MINIMAL` — personal brand, photographer, model portfolio.
- `PRODUCT CLEAN + ACCENT orange` — SaaS or fintech product site.
