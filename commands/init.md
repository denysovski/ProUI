# ProUI init

Write `PRODUCT.md` at the project root from `templates/context/PRODUCT.md`. It holds product truth that every
later design decision must respect. It does not hold colours, fonts or layout.

- Read the repository first: README, existing copy, routes, package.json, brand assets, legal pages.
- Ask at most three questions about what the code cannot tell: who the main visitor is and what they are doing,
  what makes this business different, and what must never change (name, logo, claims, legal text).
- Record the stack. On a new project ask once: plain HTML/CSS, a framework, or "you choose"; write the answer.
- Mark anything inferred as inferred. Never invent customers, prices, numbers or reviews (`CP-14`).
