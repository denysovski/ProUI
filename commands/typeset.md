# ProUI typeset

A typography pass. Load `rules/typography/README.md`.

- Make two judgements first, separately: is the face right for this subject (`TY-01`, `SL-59`), and is the
  hierarchy clear at a squint (`TY-05`, `LY-02`)?
- Then a mechanical scan: body size, measure, line height, tracking, heading ratios, weights, wrapping,
  orphans, real quotes and dashes, tabular figures, font loading and fallback metrics (`TY-06` to `TY-19`).
- Check every shipped language for glyph coverage (`TY-20`).
- Present any change of face as two or three named specimens with the real headline and a paragraph, and let
  the user choose.
