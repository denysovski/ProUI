# XSlop bolder

For a design that is correct but forgettable. Increase commitment, not decoration.

- Commit the colour: move from Restrained to Committed or Drenched (`CL-01`), one colour owning whole regions.
- Increase scale contrast: a clearly larger focal element, a steeper type ratio (`TY-05`).
- Choose a face with a point of view for display (`TY-01`, `SL-59`).
- Replace one generic section with something only this business can show (`DC-06`).
- Add one authored moment: a signature interaction or motion (`MO`), not effects everywhere.
- Do not add gradients, glows, glass or badges to look "bolder" (`SL-02` to `SL-05`, `SL-15`).
